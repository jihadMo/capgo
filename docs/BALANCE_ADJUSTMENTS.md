# Heliopoly Earth Camping Anti-Turtle Balance Adjustment

Integration guide for mitigating infinite Earth camping meta without asset holdings in Heliopoly.

## Mechanics

1. **Tax Escalation:** Increase landing tax exponentially for asset-less players camping on Earth beyond 5 consecutive turns.
2. **Win Condition Check:** Require a minimum asset net worth (> 0) to qualify for last-alive victory conditions.
